## 3. Mechanics currently live

- **Corporate Humility Score** in the top bar — *ours*, not yours, falling as the
  site gets looked at. FAQ item explains why we can't remove it.
- **Cart that shrinks** — item count stays 0, total climbs. "You have added:
  Nothing. You have added it three times."
- **Size selector** — Smaller / Smaller than that / Please stop. Choosing
  "Please stop" reverts and prints "We've stopped."
- **Testimonials redact themselves** 8s after scrolling into view — attribution
  becomes "(withdrew permission to be quoted)". Refresh restores them briefly.
- **Write-only reviews** — full form, real counter, real submit. "Your review has
  been recorded and will never be displayed."
- **Newsletter unsubscribes you on signup.**
- **Live viewer counter counts down** to "1 person is viewing this page. It's
  you. It's been you for a while."
- **Idle reward** at 90s of stillness — appears once, dismissed by any movement,
  never returns.
- **Exit-intent popup** with one button: "Wait — / No. Go ahead. / [Leave]"
- **Founder frame** — empty gilt frame, museum spotlight, guard rope, brass
  plaque reading "Oil on nothing." Insurance line is the joke's second turn.
- **Waitlist** → "You are #1. That's a problem."

### Built but NOT installed — your call

- **Visitor Humility Score that drops to 12 when you share it.** Funniest single
  mechanic in the pile, with 8 score bands ("Working On It, Audibly", "Humble In
  A Way You've Described To Others", "Would Like Credit", "Shared Your Humility
  Score"). Held out because the adversarial pass says scoring *the visitor* moves
  the target from status culture to you-specifically, which reads as grading.
  Option: run both, with ours falling faster than yours.
- **Search that returns 0 results for everything** except "nothing", "less", and
  empty string, which return Enterprise.
- **Achievement badges, all eight permanently locked.** "Quietly Did The Right
  Thing — unlocks when nobody notices. We can't detect that, so this will stay
  locked."
- **404:** "This page could not be found. In fairness, it was about us."
- **Cookie banner:** "We collect nothing. We would honestly rather not know."
- **Order confirmation:** "Your order number has been removed from our records
  and yours. The package will arrive, and you may deny that it did."

## 4. The give section — needs your two decisions

Currently at the bottom, satire fully stopped above it, plus a plain link on the
first screen so nothing is sprung on anyone.

**Decision A — which organization(s).** Skip's read: one pick reads as a
political position; three to five with the visitor choosing defuses that.

**Decision B — mechanism.** Recommended: link straight to the organization's own
donation page with anonymous giving available. We never hold funds, so no
charitable-solicitation registration, no receipts owed, and no card field on a
joke storefront (which is otherwise the literal shape of a phishing site).

Three drafts of the copy — currently running a merge of A and B:

> **A.** Everything above this line is a joke. This isn't. The button below sends
> money to people who need it, through an organization we've checked. You'll get
> no receipt with your name on it, no confirmation you can screenshot, and no
> share card — not as a bit, but because that's the only part of this whole site
> that was ever true. You'll never be able to prove you clicked it. Neither will we.

> **B.** We spent a year building a store that sells nothing so we could put one
> working button at the bottom of it. This is the button. It sends your money, in
> full, to [org]. It does not send us anything. It does not send you anything
> either — no email, no name on a wall, no badge, no thank-you page you could post.
> If you give, the only person who will ever know is you.

> **C.** One real thing, at the end. The joke was that you can't buy humility. The
> truer version is that you can buy something *with* it — you can take the money
> you'd have spent proving who you are and hand it to someone who needs it more,
> and then not mention it. Nothing comes back. No receipt, no name, no proof.
